# Off-site outreach plan

AI assistants and Google lean heavily on third-party pages when answering "best couples app" questions. This file lists the pages worth getting Lovio into, a pitch you can adapt, and listing tasks.

The targets come from web searches on 6 Nov 2026 for "best apps for couples 2026", "best couple widget apps iPhone" and "best long distance relationship apps". Rankings change, so re-check before each outreach round.

**Rules:** be honest, never pay for placement unless it's clearly labelled as sponsored, never write fake reviews, and always disclose you work on Lovio.

## 3. Pitch template (adapt per site, keep it short)

> **Subject:** A couples app for your "{article title}" round-up
>
> Hi {name},
>
> I work on Lovio, a couples app for iPhone and Android, and I enjoyed your piece on {topic}. I noticed it covers {an app they list} for {use case}; Lovio might be a useful addition for readers who want {their use case — e.g. "widgets and things to do together in one app"}.
>
> In one sentence: Lovio pairs you with your partner and gives you shared Home Screen widgets (including one that shows doodles from your partner), a daily question you both answer, date ideas for couples near and far, and eight two-player games. It's free to download, and one Premium subscription covers both partners.
>
> Everything you'd need, including facts, icons and contact details, is at https://getlovio.app/press/. Happy to give you Premium to try it with your partner.
>
> Thanks either way,
> {your name}, Lovio (FOURPLEX SIA)

Only offer free Premium if you're actually willing to give it; publishers that accept it should disclose it.

## 4. Short videos (Google's AI features cite YouTube)

Make 15–30-second vertical screen recordings, posted to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels:
1. Drawing a doodle → it appearing on your partner's Canvas widget (two phones side by side).
2. "Long-distance couple routine": morning doodle, the day's question, an evening game.
3. Each Arcade game in 5 seconds.
4. How to add a Lovio widget (matches the guide on the site).

Use the same title words people search for: "couples widget", "drawing widget for couples", "long distance app".

## 5. Communities

Founders may answer genuine questions in r/LongDistance, r/relationships, r/iphone widget threads and similar. Read each subreddit's self-promotion rules first, always say you work on Lovio, and only mention it where it actually answers the question. No sock-puppet accounts and no paid posts.
