Common
questions

How do I pair with my partner?

One of you shares the 6-character invite code from the app; the other enters it (or opens the invite link) and you're paired. You can pair across iPhone and Android.

My partner's answers aren't showing up

Make sure you're both on the latest version of Lovio and connected to the internet, then pull down on the Home screen to refresh. Still stuck? Email us and we'll take a look.

How do I add a Lovio widget to my home screen?

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen, tap the “+” (iPhone) or “Widgets” (Android), search for Lovio and choose the widget you like — Memories, Days Together, Live Distance or Canvas.

How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are handled by your app store. On iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the Profile tab in Lovio and choose Delete Account. If you can't access the app, email support@getlovio.app from your registered address. See our Privacy Policy for details.
