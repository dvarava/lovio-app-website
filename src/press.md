## Lovio in one sentence

> Lovio is a couples app for iPhone and Android: shared Home Screen widgets, a daily question, date ideas, memories and two-player games, synced between partners.

## Lovio in a paragraph

> Lovio is the couples app for every single day. Partners pair with a 6-character code, then share Home Screen widgets — Days Together, Memories, Live Distance and a Canvas widget that shows doodles from your partner. Each day brings a new Daily Spark question that both answer before revealing. Couples can swipe through date ideas together (with options for long-distance couples), keep a shared bucket list and memory scrapbook, play couples quizzes, and play eight two-player games in the Arcade. Lovio is free to download, and one Lovio Premium subscription covers both partners. It's made by FOURPLEX SIA in Latvia.
