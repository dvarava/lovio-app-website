## Where Locket is the better choice

- **You want to share with more than one person.** Locket lets you add up to 20 friends, so your partner, your best friend and your family can all be on it.
- **You mostly want photos.** Snap, send, and it's on their Home Screen — Locket keeps it simple.
- **You like recaps.** Locket builds a history of shared photos and makes a recap video each month.
- **Your friends are already on it.** It's one of the most widely used widget apps, with a very large number of App Store ratings.

## Where Lovio fits better

- **It's just the two of you.** Lovio pairs you with one partner — nothing to manage, nobody else in the feed.
- **More than photos.** Alongside the Memories widget, there's a [drawing widget](/features/drawing-widget/), a Live Distance widget and a Days Together counter.
- **Things to do together.** A [daily question](/features/daily-questions/), date ideas you swipe through together, a shared bucket list, quizzes and [eight two-player games](/features/couples-games/).

## Can you use both?

Yes, and plenty of couples will. Locket is great for sharing photos with your wider circle; Lovio is for the two of you. If you only want one app for your relationship, Lovio covers photos plus a lot more; if you only want photo-sharing with lots of people, Locket is the simpler choice.
