## Where Widgetable is the better choice

Widgetable is one of the most popular social widget apps on the App Store, with a very large number of ratings. It shines if:

- **You want widgets with your friends too.** It's designed for groups of "besties", not just couples.
- **You like virtual pets and plants.** Co-parenting a pet or growing a garden on your screen is a big part of the app.
- **You want to share moods and daily status.** Mood Bubble, Mood Jar, the Sleep Widget and status widgets have no equivalent in Lovio.
- **You love decorating your phone.** It has wallpapers and themes to match your widgets.

## Where Lovio fits better

Lovio is built for one relationship, and the widgets are only part of it:

- **Everything is for the two of you.** You pair with one partner, so there are no group chats or friend lists to manage.
- **More to do together than widgets.** A [daily question](/features/daily-questions/) you both answer, date ideas you swipe through together, a shared bucket list and a memory scrapbook.
- **Games for two.** The [Arcade](/features/couples-games/) has eight two-player games you can play live or take turns at.
- **One subscription for both of you.** Lovio Premium covers both partners.

## Who should choose which?

- **Mostly want cute, playful widgets with friends and a partner →** Widgetable.
- **Want a couples app with widgets, questions, dates and games in one place →** Lovio.
- **Can't decide?** Both are free to download, so try them together and keep the one you both open more.
