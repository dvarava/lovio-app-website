## A few rules for good couple doodles

- **Make it about them.** Their favourite snack beats a generic heart.
- **Keep it fast.** Ten seconds of effort, sent often, beats one big drawing a month.
- **Use words.** A drawing plus two or three words is clearer than a drawing alone.
- **Embrace the wobble.** Bad drawings are part of the charm. Nobody needs a perfect circle.

## Drawing games to play together

Turn doodling into a game:

- **Finish my drawing.** One of you draws half of something; the other finishes it.
- **Guess the doodle.** Draw something and make your partner guess what it is (or what it was supposed to be).
- **Draw from memory.** Both draw the same thing — your first date, your partner's face, your flat — and compare.
- **One-line challenge.** Draw something without lifting your finger.
- **Daily theme.** Pick a theme for the week ("animals", "food", "things I love about you") and send one doodle a day.
