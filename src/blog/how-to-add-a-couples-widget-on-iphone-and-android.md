<div class="c-quick"><p><b>Quick answer:</b> On iPhone, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap <em>Edit</em> → <em>Add Widget</em>, search for the app and tap <em>Add Widget</em>. On Android, touch and hold an empty spot, tap <em>Widgets</em>, find the app, then touch and hold a widget and drag it into place. Both of you need the app installed and paired for a couples widget to show shared content.</p></div>

The steps below use Lovio as the example, but they work the same for any widget app. Menu names can vary slightly between iOS versions and Android phone makers.

## Before you start

- **Both of you need the app.** Couples widgets show things your partner shares — photos, doodles, notes — so you both need the app installed and paired.
- **Open the app once.** Widgets often don't appear in the widget list until you've opened the app at least once.
- **Update your phone if it's very old.** Lovio needs iOS 16 or later on iPhone.

## Add a widget to your iPhone Home Screen

<ol class="c-steps">
<li><strong>Touch and hold</strong> an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps start to jiggle.</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Edit</strong> in the top-left corner, then <strong>Add Widget</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Search</strong> for the app (for example, "Lovio") and tap it.</li>
<li><strong>Swipe</strong> left and right to see the available widgets and sizes.</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Add Widget</strong>, drag it where you want it, then tap <strong>Done</strong>.</li>
</ol>

**Tip:** you can stack widgets on top of each other to save space — drag one widget onto another of the same size. Swipe up or down on the stack to switch between them.

## Add a widget to your iPhone Lock Screen

Lock Screen widgets are smaller and simpler, but you see them every time you pick up your phone.

<ol class="c-steps">
<li><strong>Unlock</strong> your iPhone with Face ID or Touch ID, but stay on the Lock Screen.</li>
<li><strong>Touch and hold</strong> the Lock Screen, then tap <strong>Customize</strong> and choose <strong>Lock Screen</strong>.</li>
<li>Tap the <strong>widget area</strong> below the clock.</li>
<li>Find the app in the list and tap the widget you want to add.</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Done</strong>.</li>
</ol>

Not every app offers Lock Screen widgets, and they only show what fits in a small space.

## Add a widget on Android

<ol class="c-steps">
<li><strong>Touch and hold</strong> an empty spot on your Home Screen.</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Widgets</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Scroll or search</strong> for the app (for example, "Lovio").</li>
<li><strong>Touch and hold</strong> the widget you want, then drag it onto your Home Screen and let go.</li>
<li>If the widget can be resized, touch and hold it, then drag the handles.</li>
</ol>

On Samsung Galaxy phones the button may say **Widgets** at the bottom of the screen after you touch and hold; on Pixel phones it appears in a small pop-up menu.

## When a widget won't update

Widgets don't refresh constantly — your phone decides when to update them to save battery. If a widget looks stuck:

1. **Open the app.** Opening it gives the widget a chance to refresh.
2. **Check your connection.** Couples widgets need the internet to fetch what your partner sent.
3. **Turn off Low Power Mode / Battery Saver.** Power-saving modes slow down background updates.
4. **Allow background activity.** On iPhone, check Settings → General → Background App Refresh. On Android, check that battery optimisation isn't restricting the app.
5. **Remove and re-add the widget.** It's quick and often fixes a widget that's stuck on old content.
6. **Restart your phone.** The classic fix, for a reason.

If none of that helps, it's worth contacting the app's support team — for Lovio, that's [support@getlovio.app](mailto:support@getlovio.app).

## Which widget should you add first?

If you're not sure, start with the one you'll want to see most:

- **Live apart?** A distance or drawing widget makes the gap feel smaller.
- **Love photos?** A memories widget keeps your favourite moment on screen.
- **Just starting out?** A days-together counter is a sweet way to mark the milestones.
