<div class="c-quick"><p><b>Quick answer:</b> For couples, the best phone games are ones you can play <em>with each other</em> rather than just next to each other: co-op puzzles, quick head-to-head games, word games and drawing-and-guessing games. Pick turn-based games if your schedules don't line up, and real-time games for date nights.</p></div>

## What makes a good game for couples

- **Cross-platform.** If one of you has an iPhone and the other has Android, check that the game supports both.
- **Turn-based or live.** Turn-based games fit busy weeks and different time zones. Live games are better for date nights.
- **Short rounds.** Five-minute games are easier to fit into a day than hour-long sessions.
- **Co-op as well as competitive.** Competition is fun until it isn't. Having games where you're on the same team keeps things friendly.
- **Free to try.** Check what's free and what needs a purchase before you both download something.

## Types of phone games couples enjoy

### Co-op puzzle games
You work on the same puzzle together. They're relaxed, good for unwinding after work, and nobody has to lose.

### Quick head-to-head games
Short, competitive rounds — think air hockey, connect-four style strategy, or speed challenges. Great for a bit of playful trash talk.

### Word games
Classic crossword and word-building games where you take turns. Ideal for couples who like a slow, thoughtful game over a few days.

### Drawing and guessing games
One of you draws, the other guesses. They tend to produce the biggest laughs because nobody is as good at drawing as they think.

### Quizzes and question games
Less "game", more conversation: answer the same question or quiz and compare. You'll learn things about each other that don't come up in everyday chat. Our [daily question for couples](/features/daily-questions/) works like this.

### Classic board games, online
Many familiar board and card games have apps that let you play online with a friend — useful for long-distance couples who used to play in person.

## Games you can play with no app at all

Just open your messages:

1. **Twenty questions.** One thinks of something; the other has twenty yes/no questions.
2. **Would you rather.** Take turns asking. Keep it silly or make it deep.
3. **Emoji films.** Describe a film in emojis; the other guesses.
4. **Two truths and a lie.** Surprisingly revealing, even after years together.
5. **One-line story.** Write a story together, one sentence each.
6. **Song lyric chain.** Reply with a lyric that starts with the last word of theirs.
7. **Rate my day.** Send a photo of your day; they rate it out of 10 with a reason.
8. **Hot takes.** Each send an opinion; the other agrees or disagrees and explains.
