<div class="c-quick"><p><b>Quick answer:</b> Good long-distance dates have a shared activity, not just conversation: cook the same recipe on a call, watch a film in sync, play an online game, do a virtual tour, or plan your next visit together. If your time zones barely overlap, do "same thing, different time" dates like reading the same book or sending each other photo diaries.</p></div>

## How to make a virtual date feel like a date

- **Schedule it.** Put it in both calendars with a start time in each of your time zones.
- **Set the scene.** Candles, a nice shirt, a tidy background. It sounds silly; it works.
- **Do something together.** Shared activity is what turns a call into a date.
- **Put other apps away.** Agree on no scrolling during the date.
- **End with a plan.** Decide the next date before you hang up.

## If you overlap for an hour or more

These work best live, on a video call.

1. **Cook the same recipe.** Pick something with similar ingredients in both countries and cook it at the same time.
2. **Synced movie night.** Press play at the same moment (count down from three) or use a watch-party tool, and keep the call on in the background.
3. **Two-player games.** Play an online board game, a co-op video game or a couples game app. Our [games for couples on your phone](/blog/games-to-play-with-your-partner-on-your-phone/) guide has ideas.
4. **Virtual museum tour.** Many big museums offer free online tours and collections. Explore one together and pick your favourite piece.
5. **Taste test.** Each buy the same three snacks or drinks and rate them together.
6. **Paint or draw the same thing.** Set a timer, draw each other, and reveal.
7. **Online class for two.** A dance, language, cooking or drawing class you both join.
8. **Quiz night.** Answer a couples quiz or a set of deep questions and compare answers.
9. **Plan your next trip.** Open a map and plan your next visit in detail — where you'll eat, what you'll do.
10. **Stargazing call.** Find the moon at the same time (even if it's at a different time of day for one of you) and talk about your days.
11. **Karaoke.** Take turns choosing songs. Bonus points for duets.
12. **Build a playlist.** Take turns adding songs that remind you of each other, then listen through together.

## If your time zones barely overlap

"Same thing, different time" dates — you each do your part when it suits you.

13. **Book club for two.** Read the same chapter each week and leave voice notes about it.
14. **Photo diary.** Send each other one photo every hour you're awake for a day.
15. **Same recipe, different meals.** One of you makes it for breakfast, the other for dinner. Compare results.
16. **A daily question.** Answer the same question each day and reveal your answers when you're both awake. (Lovio's [Daily Spark](/features/daily-questions/) is built for this.)
17. **Doodle exchange.** Leave a drawing for them to wake up to. Need inspiration? See our [drawing ideas for couples](/blog/drawing-ideas-for-couples/).
18. **Turn-based games.** Word games and board games where you take turns at your own pace.
19. **Walk "together".** Take a walk in your city, record a voice note or take photos along the way, and send it to them.
20. **Shared watchlist.** Each watch an episode on your own and send your reactions.
21. **Letter writing.** Real letters on paper. They take time to arrive, which is part of the point.

## Free (or nearly free)

22. **Show-and-tell.** Show each other something you love — a book, a jumper, a childhood photo — and tell the story behind it.
23. **Sunset swap.** Send each other a photo of your sky at sunset.
24. **Make a bucket list.** Write down what you want to do together when you're in the same place, and keep adding to it.
25. **Memory lane.** Go through old photos together and tell each other what you remember.
26. **Fake restaurant.** One of you is the waiter, the other the guest. Ridiculous; highly recommended.
27. **"Get ready with me".** Video call while you both get ready for the day or for bed.

## When you want to make an effort

28. **Surprise delivery.** Order their favourite food to their door and eat together on a call.
29. **Care package.** Fill a box with snacks, a photo, a handwritten note and something that smells like you.
30. **Matching items.** Buy the same mug, candle or jumper and use them on your next call.
31. **Scavenger hunt.** Hide notes around their place before you leave after a visit, and send clues later.
32. **Book a class in advance.** A paid online cooking or wine class with a kit posted to each of you.
33. **Countdown calendar.** Make them an advent-style countdown to your next visit.

## Dates for special days

34. **Anniversary recreation.** Recreate your first date — the same food, the same playlist, the same film.
35. **Birthday on a call.** Send a cake (or the ingredients) and sing badly at the same time.
