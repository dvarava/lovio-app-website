## What Lovio is for

Relationships are mostly made of ordinary days. Lovio is built for those: a small question to answer over coffee, a doodle between meetings, a date idea you both swiped right on, a game before bed. None of it takes long, and all of it is shared between the two of you.

## What's in the app

- **[Couples widgets](/features/couples-widgets/)** — Days Together, Memories, Canvas and Live Distance, on your Home Screen.
- **[A drawing widget](/features/drawing-widget/)** — doodle in Lovio, and it shows up on your partner's Canvas widget.
- **[Daily Spark](/features/daily-questions/)** — one new question a day; you both answer, then reveal.
- **Date ideas** — swipe through ideas together, including options for long-distance couples, and save favourites to a shared bucket list.
- **Sticky notes** — short messages that show up for your partner.
- **Memory scrapbook** — turn your dates into a shared journal with photos and notes.
- **[The Arcade](/features/couples-games/)** — eight two-player games, plus couples quiz packs.
- **Us Streak** — a daily streak you keep together.

Lovio is free to download on the [App Store](/go/ios?placement=about) and [Google Play](/go/android?placement=about). Lovio Premium unlocks extra content, and one subscription covers both partners.

## Who makes Lovio

Lovio is a product of **FOURPLEX SIA**, a company registered in Latvia. FOURPLEX SIA is the developer listed on the App Store and Google Play.

## Your data

Your answers, notes, drawings and memories are shared with your linked partner. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) explains what we collect and how we handle it, and our [Terms of Service](/terms.html) cover how Lovio can be used.
