## What each Lovio widget shows

Each widget is a small window onto something you share. Add one, add all of them, or mix sizes — whatever fits your Home Screen.

### Days Together
A counter for how long you've been together, with both of your faces on it. It's a quiet reminder that turns into a small celebration on round numbers — day 100, day 365, day 1,000.

### Memories
Shows your latest photo memory from your shared Lovio scrapbook. Add a photo from a date and it becomes the picture you both see every time you unlock your phone.

### Canvas (the drawing widget)
Draw something in Lovio and it appears on your partner's Canvas widget. A heart, a cat, a terrible portrait of them — the point is that it's from you. See [how the drawing widget works](/features/drawing-widget/).

### Live Distance
Shows how far apart the two of you are. It's the widget long-distance couples tend to love most: the number shrinking on the day you travel to see each other is hard to beat.

## How to add a Lovio widget

<ol class="c-steps">
<li><strong>Install Lovio and pair up.</strong> One of you shares a 6-character invite code; the other enters it.</li>
<li><strong>Open the widget gallery.</strong> On iPhone, touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap <em>Edit</em>, then <em>Add Widget</em>. On Android, touch and hold an empty spot and tap <em>Widgets</em>.</li>
<li><strong>Find Lovio and pick a widget.</strong> Search for "Lovio", swipe through the widget types and sizes, then add the one you want.</li>
<li><strong>Ask your partner to do the same.</strong> The fun is in both of you seeing it.</li>
</ol>

There's a longer walkthrough, with fixes for widgets that won't update, in our guide on [how to add a couples widget on iPhone and Android](/blog/how-to-add-a-couples-widget-on-iphone-and-android/).

## Why widgets work for couples

Messages need a reply. Widgets don't. They sit on the screen you already look at dozens of times a day and show you something about the other person without asking anything of you. That makes them a low-effort way to feel connected on busy days — and a lot of couples find that small, frequent reminders matter more than big gestures.

## More than a widget app

The widgets are the part of Lovio you see most, but they're connected to the rest of the app: the photos come from your shared scrapbook, and the doodles come from the canvas you both draw on. Lovio also has a [daily question for the two of you](/features/daily-questions/), date ideas you swipe through together, quizzes, and [eight two-player games](/features/couples-games/).
