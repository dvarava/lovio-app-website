## Play live or take turns

Some evenings you're both on the sofa with your phones. Other weeks one of you is on night shifts or eight hours ahead. The Arcade works for both: play in real time when you're together, or take your turn when you have a minute and let your partner take theirs later.

## Quizzes, too

Alongside the Arcade, Lovio has couples quiz packs that range from flirty to deep — "Green flag or red flag?", "Why I love you", "If we had one night" and more. You both answer, then see how well you know each other.

## Keep the streak alive

Lovio's **Us Streak** is a daily streak you keep together. It's a small nudge to check in, and if life gets in the way and you miss a day, you can bring it back.
