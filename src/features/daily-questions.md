## How the Daily Spark works

<ol class="c-steps">
<li><strong>A new question arrives.</strong> Both of you get the same question each day.</li>
<li><strong>You each answer.</strong> Write as much or as little as you like, whenever you have a minute.</li>
<li><strong>Reveal.</strong> Once you've both answered, you see each other's answers.</li>
<li><strong>Talk about it.</strong> The best answers usually start a conversation — over dinner, on a call, or by text.</li>
</ol>

## Why one question a day works

Long conversation-starter lists are great for a road trip, but hard to keep up with day to day. One question is small enough to answer in a queue for coffee, and because you both answer before you see each other's, you get honest answers instead of "same as you". Over weeks and months, those small answers add up to a lot of things you might never have thought to ask.

## Example questions

A few questions in the same spirit, written for this page (the app has its own):

- What's a small thing I do that always makes your day?
- Which meal would you want us to learn to cook together?
- What's a memory of us you think about more than I'd guess?
- If we had a free Saturday with no plans, what would your perfect version look like?
- What's something you were scared of as a kid that seems funny now?
- What's one thing you'd like us to do more of this year?
- Which song instantly reminds you of us?
- What's a compliment you've received that you still remember?
- Where's somewhere you'd love to take me that I've never been?
- What's something you're proud of this week that you haven't told me?
