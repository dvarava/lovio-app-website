## What long distance actually needs

Most long-distance couples don't struggle with the big moments — the visits, the reunions, the long calls. They struggle with the gaps in between: the days when you're both busy, the hours when one of you is asleep, the feeling that you're missing the ordinary bits of each other's life. Lovio is built for those gaps.

## How Lovio helps when you're apart

### Something of theirs on your Home Screen
[Lovio's widgets](/features/couples-widgets/) show your days together, your latest shared photo, how far apart you are, and your partner's newest doodle — right on the screen you look at all day. It's a low-effort way to feel like they're around.

### Doodles across time zones
With the [Canvas drawing widget](/features/drawing-widget/), you can leave a drawing before you go to sleep and it'll be waiting on their Home Screen when they wake up.

### A daily question, answered on your own time
The [Daily Spark](/features/daily-questions/) gives you both the same question each day. You answer when it suits you, then reveal each other's answers — no need to be awake at the same time.

### Date ideas with a long-distance filter
Lovio's date ideas let you swipe through options together and match on the ones you both like, and there's a long-distance option for when you can't be in the same room. Save the ones you'll do when you're together to your shared bucket list.

### Games you can play from anywhere
The [Arcade](/features/couples-games/) has eight two-player games you can play live or take turns at.

## A simple long-distance routine

One routine that works for a lot of couples:

1. **Morning:** send a quick doodle — a coffee cup, a sun, "gm".
2. **Lunch:** answer the day's question when you have a minute.
3. **Evening:** reveal each other's answers and talk about them on your call.
4. **Weekend:** pick a long-distance date idea and do it together.
