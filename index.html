FAQ

Good to know.

Anything else? We're at support@getlovio.app.

Do we both need the app?

Yes — Lovio is made for two. One of you shares a 6-character invite code, the other enters it, and you're paired.

Is Lovio free?

Lovio is free to download. Lovio Premium unlocks every quiz pack, every Arcade game and more — and one subscription covers both of you.

Can I pair an iPhone with an Android?

Yes. Lovio works on iPhone and Android, so you can pair no matter which phone your partner uses.

Who can see our answers and photos?

Your answers, notes, drawings and memories are shared with your linked partner. Read our Privacy Policy to see how we handle your data.
